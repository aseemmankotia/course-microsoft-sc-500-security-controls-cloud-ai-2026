# Chapter 2 Lab: Privileged Access: PIM, Managed Identities & RBAC

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Configure a PIM eligible assignment for the Global Administrator role and configure a user-assigned managed identity for an Azure VM to access Key Vault without stored credentials

## Key takeaway

PIM and managed identities together minimize standing privilege and credential sprawl; the exam tests choosing the right identity type and activation model per scenario.
