## Chapter 12: Full Practice Exam Simulation & Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Total exam time and question count used for pacing this course's practice exam simulation? | 120 minutes, 40-60 questions. Exam tip: divide remaining time by remaining questions continuously, not just once at the start. |
| 2 | How much time buffer should you reserve for final review before submitting? | 5-10 minutes minimum. Exam tip: this buffer is for revisiting flagged questions and reviewing your historically weakest domain first. |
| 3 | Approx. time budget for one case study with 5-6 embedded questions? | ~20-25 minutes total (skim + targeted re-reads + answering). Exam tip: case studies cost more per-question time than standalone items — budget accordingly. |
| 4 | Case study reading strategy: Skim vs. Targeted Re-read — when to use each? | Skim once at the start for overview/key facts. Targeted re-read only the passage relevant to the current question. Never fully re-read the whole case study per question. |
| 5 | Best practice when you cannot fully analyze a flagged question near time expiry? | Eliminate obviously wrong options fast, guess among the rest, and always submit an answer. Blank answers are always scored wrong — a guess has nonzero expected value. |
| 6 | Review priority order when time is limited during final review pass? | 1) Flagged questions in your weakest domain, 2) other flagged questions, 3) confident answers last (if time remains). Weak-domain-first maximizes expected point gain. |
| 7 | Elimination strategy: first thing to remove from a 4-option scenario question? | Options referencing deprecated features, wrong portals, or services that don't fit the requirement type. This narrows choices before deeper comparison against scenario requirements. |
| 8 | Sentinel vs. Defender for Cloud — quick exam-elimination rule? | Sentinel = cross-workload SIEM, correlation, long-term retention. Defender for Cloud = posture management + workload protection. If the scenario says 'centralized alert correlation across hybrid/multi-cloud,' pick Sentinel. |
| 9 | Drag-and-drop/ordering questions: how to handle an uncertain step's position? | Anchor the steps you're sure of first, then use dependency logic (what must precede what) for the uncertain one. Never leave the list in default/unordered state — that is a guaranteed-wrong answer. |
| 10 | What does 'flagging' a question do during the exam, and when should you use it? | Marks a question for later review without losing your current selection. Use it immediately when uncertain — don't spend extra time deliberating on first pass; move on and revisit if time allows. |
| 11 | Common exam-taking mistake: judging correctness by answer length? | False heuristic — answer length/detail has no correlation with correctness on scenario questions. Judge only against stated scenario requirements. |
| 12 | Rule for unanswered/blank questions at submission time? | Always scored as incorrect — never leave a question blank. A fast elimination + best guess always has higher expected value than leaving it blank. |
